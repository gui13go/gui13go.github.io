About Tonight's Movie

GNU, Linux & The Free Software Movement

Revolution OS chronicles the true history of the hacker visionaries who rebelled against proprietary software monopolies, creating GNU, Linux, and the Open Source movement.

Featuring candid interviews with Richard Stallman, Linus Torvalds, Eric S. Raymond, Bruce Perens, and the pioneers of the digital commons.

Everything in Chronological Perspective

Revolution OS jumps between philosophical debates, technical breakthroughs, and Wall Street frenzies. Follow along on the website to see the complete timeline.

1983 · Phase 1
Richard Stallman & GNU

The philosophical declaration that code should be free to read, study, modify, and share. The launch of the GNU Project and GPL.

1991 · Phase 2
Linus Torvalds & Linux

A university student in Helsinki posts a hobby kernel to Usenet. The missing piece connects with GNU tools to create a viable operating system.

1998 · Phase 3
Open Source Initiative

Netscape releases source code. Eric S. Raymond and Bruce Perens coin 'Open Source' to make software freedom commercially appealing to enterprise.

1999 · Phase 4
Wall Street IPO Mania

Red Hat and VA Linux smash stock market records. Volunteer hackers suddenly find themselves at the epicenter of Silicon Valley capitalism.

Join the Discussion: Overcoming Shyness

Movie Night is not a lecture — it is a casual, judgment-free space to ask questions, react, and practice voicing your thoughts.

Open Floor · Everyone Welcome
🎙️

Guest Commentators

Faculty & Expert Commentary
Prof. Dirk Klann: Academic reflections, historical milestones, and broader implications of technology on society.
Mr. Yanhuang Xie: Engineering realities, industry ecosystems, and the practical value of Open Source in modern tech.
All Faculty & Mentors: We warmly invite teachers and advisors in the room to share their perspectives and field student questions!
💡 "Commentary is an invitation to converse, not a final verdict."
🌱

To All Students

Overcoming the Shyness Barrier
Zero Prerequisites: You do NOT need to be a computer science major or coder to participate. Intuitions and reactions are gold!
No Silly Questions: Every great discussion begins with curiosity. If something in the film puzzled you, others are wondering too!
Safe Practice Ground: Speaking in front of peers and professors is a skill. Use this relaxed room to take your first step!
✨ 勇敢开麦： 这是属于每个人的轻松沙龙，没有标准答案，尽情表达你的直觉与思考！
🔥

Icebreaker Prompts

Topics to Spark Thoughts
Philosophy vs. Business: Did you agree more with Stallman's moral crusade or Raymond's commercial pragmatism?
The Power of Fun: Linus started Linux 'just for fun'. How does passion compare against corporate funding?
In the Age of AI: With closed AI vs. open weights today, are we reliving the exact battle seen in Revolution OS?
💬 "Feel free to disagree, question, or share an unexpected observation!"
