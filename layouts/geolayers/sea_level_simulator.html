Methodology & Hydrological Modeling

Unlike simplistic “bathtub” models that flood all terrestrial pixels below a given elevation regardless of inland topography, this visualization employs SCALGO’s hydrological flow engine. A terrestrial cell is only marked flooded if it is hydrologically connected to the open sea or ocean basin without natural or artificial terrain barriers blocking water intrusion.

Elevation Source (SRTM 90m v4.1)

Shuttle Radar Topography Mission (SRTM) digital elevation model provided by the CGIAR Consortium for Spatial Information (CGIAR-CSI). Provides continuous global topographical coverage between 60°N and 56°S latitude at 3 arc-second (approx. 90-meter) resolution.

WMTS Tile Service & EPSG:3857

Tiles are requested dynamically over the Web Map Tile Service (WMTS) specification in Web Mercator projection across zoom levels 0 through 14. Modifying the sea-level threshold sends updated dimension query keys to the tile generator to refresh inundation contours in real time.
