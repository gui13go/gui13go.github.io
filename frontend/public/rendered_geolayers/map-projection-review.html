Understanding Map Projections

Map projections transform the spherical three-dimensional surface of the Earth onto a two-dimensional planar medium. Carl Friedrich Gauss proved with his Theorema Egregium that a sphere cannot be projected onto a plane without introducing distortion. Consequently, every map projection represents a compromise among preserving area (equivalence), conformal angles and local shapes (orthomorphism), distances from specific origins (equidistance), or navigational directions (loxodromes and great circles).
