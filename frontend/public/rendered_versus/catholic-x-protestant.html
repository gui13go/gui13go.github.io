Catholic x Protestant

Christian Denominations
Corner A

Catholic Apostolic Origins (Rome)

Roman Catholicism. The historic Western Christian Church led by the Bishop of Rome (The Pope) through Apostolic Succession. Centered on the seven sacraments, sacred tradition alongside scripture, and transubstantiation in the Holy Eucharist.

VS
Corner B

Protestant The Reformation (1517+)

Protestantism (sparked by Martin Luther's 95 Theses in 1517 and John Calvin). Reformed Christianity centered on the Five Solas: Sola Scriptura (Bible alone as supreme authority) and Sola Fide (Justification by faith alone, rejecting papal authority and purgatory).

Key Differences & Divergences

  • Spiritual Authority: The Pope and Magisterium alongside Scripture and Sacred Tradition (Catholic) VS Sola Scriptura—the Bible alone interpreted by individual believers (Protestant).
  • Salvation: Faith working through love and participating in sacramental grace (Catholic) VS Imputed righteousness through faith alone without works (Protestant).
  • The Eucharist: Literal transubstantiation of bread and wine into the actual body and blood of Christ VS Consubstantiation, spiritual presence, or symbolic memorial.
  • Historical Wars: The Thirty Years' War (1618–1648), French Wars of Religion, and the Peace of Westphalia that reshaped the map of modern Europe.

Common Ground

Both confess the historic Christian Creeds (Nicene, Apostles'), worshipping Jesus Christ as the Son of God and Savior of humanity.

The Verdict / Outcome

“Catholicism remains the world's largest unified Christian body (1.3 billion); Protestantism ignited modern individualism, literacy, capitalism, and democracy.”
