LBJ x RFK

The Blood Feud
Corner A

Lyndon B. Johnson

The Master of the Senate. Coarse, domineering, manipulative, and a political genius. He knew how to twist arms to pass the Civil Rights Act and Build the 'Great Society'. Insecure and resentful of the Kennedys.

VS
Corner B

Robert F. Kennedy

The Heir. Intense, moody, charismatic, and initially ruthless ('The Runt'). He evolved into a crusader for the poor and marginalized. Disdained LBJ as an uncouth usurper of his brother's legacy.

Key Differences & Divergences

  • Style: Texas Bully vs. Harvard Elite.
  • Politics: Backroom deals vs. Public inspiration.
  • Vietnam: The Hawk (LBJ) vs. The Dove (RFK).
  • Core: 'politics is the art of the possible' vs. 'dream things that never were'.

Common Ground

Both Democrats who hated poverty and pushed civil rights, despite hating each other.

The Verdict / Outcome

“Mutually Assured Destruction. LBJ's presidency broke on Vietnam; RFK was assassinated before he could replace him.”
