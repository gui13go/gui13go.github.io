Optimus Prime x Megatron

The Cybertronian Civil War
Corner A

Optimus Prime

Leader of the Autobots. 'Freedom is the right of all sentient beings'. Courageous, self-sacrificing, stoic father figure. Transforms into a truck (working class/service).

VS
Corner B

Megatron

Leader of the Decepticons. 'Peace through Tyranny'. Ruthless, power-hungry gladiator. transform into a gun or tank (war/destruction).

Key Differences & Divergences

  • Ideology: Freedom vs. Order/Tyranny.
  • Origin: Librarian/Laborer (Orion Pax) vs. Gladiator.
  • Goal: Protect Life vs. Conquer the Universe.
  • Alt Mode: Truck (Builder) vs. Weapon (Destroyer).

Common Ground

Brothers in arms who became bitter enemies. Both want to save Cybertron (in their own way).

The Verdict / Outcome

“Optimus Prime. He dies (a lot), but he always returns. Megatron's tyranny is self-defeating.”
